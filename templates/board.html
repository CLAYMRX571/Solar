We are proud to introduce the esteemed members of the Board of Directors of the International Solar Energy Society. This dedicated group of volunteers represents a diverse and experienced range of professionals, united by a shared commitment to advancing solar energy innovation and sustainability across the globe.

Our board members are experts from various fields, including renewable energy, engineering, policy, research, and environmental advocacy. With a wealth of experience spanning continents, they bring invaluable global perspectives and insights to guide the Society’s mission of accelerating the adoption of solar energy worldwide.

As volunteers, each director is driven by a passion for creating a sustainable future and contributing to the growth of solar energy solutions. Together, they lead with a vision to not only meet today’s energy challenges but also to shape a cleaner, greener future for generations to come.

We are honored to have such an accomplished and dedicated group of individuals steering our efforts as we continue to champion solar energy as a key to a sustainable, carbon-neutral world.
